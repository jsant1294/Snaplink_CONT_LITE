## Observable code-level change

Before: mixed cream, ivory, paper, sand, walnut, clay, taupe, olive, and one-off hex values created inconsistent hierarchy and low-contrast copy.

After: page/surface/border/text/action roles are centralized and shared across all public Southline surfaces, with dedicated on-dark and accessible gold-text roles.
