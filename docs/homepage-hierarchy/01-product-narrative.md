# Product Narrative

The homepage now moves through seven stages:

1. Inspire the homeowner.
2. Help them find a home or place to stay.
3. Help them improve or maintain the property.
4. Connect them with trusted professionals.
5. Help them plan and act.
6. Establish community and marketplace trust.
7. Introduce the wider SnapLink network.

Southline value is complete before any broader-network cross-promotion appears.
