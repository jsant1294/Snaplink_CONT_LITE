## Recommended monetization strategy

1. Best immediate offer: a contractor lead-generation package with profile publishing and lead intake.
2. Best monthly subscription: a professional subscription bundle combining workspace access, profile visibility, and lead management.
3. Best setup-fee service: profile setup and launch packaging for contractors.
4. Best featured-listing offer: paid profile prominence for high-intent categories.
5. Best SnapLink cross-sell: a campaign or Flipbook add-on tied to the contractor profile.
6. Best real-estate monetization path: agency or brokerage operations package for CRM workflows and transaction management.
7. Best later-stage marketplace revenue model: featured placement plus premium marketplace access or higher-tier brokerage plans.
