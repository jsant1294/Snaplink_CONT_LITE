## Recommendations

- Best first professional market: remodelers.
- Best second professional market: painters or landscapers.
- Best homeowner audience: homeowners planning repairs or remodels with a specific service need.
- Best real-estate audience: small to mid-sized brokerages or agent teams.
- Best market for NFC or QR: local service contractors with high trust and repeat needs.
- Best market for subscription revenue: home-service contractors.
- Market that should wait: broad general local business categories with weak fit and higher onboarding friction.
