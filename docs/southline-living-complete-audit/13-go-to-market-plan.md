# Go-to-Market Plan

## Positioning

Southline Living is positioned as a local-services and real-estate marketplace platform that helps homeowners discover professionals and helps contractors manage intake and presence.

## Core offer

A contractor package that includes:

- public professional profile,
- local discovery presence,
- lead capture,
- simple dashboard management,
- optional campaign or Flipbook add-on.

## Packaging

- Starter: profile + lead capture + basic dashboard.
- Growth: starter plus campaign or Flipbook add-on.
- Pro: growth plus more advanced module unlocks and onboarding support.

## Sales motion

1. Identify a local professional category.
2. Offer a setup package.
3. Publish the profile and lead flow.
4. Enable one add-on.
5. Measure inbound lead quality and conversion.

## Customer onboarding

- collect service categories,
- publish business profile,
- add available contact path,
- enable the booking flow,
- review initial lead intake,
- activate one premium module.

## Success metrics

- number of profiles published,
- number of leads received,
- conversion of leads into booked jobs,
- profile activation rate,
- add-on attach rate,
- churn by channel.

## Launch priority

The first launch should target a small number of home-service categories where the product is already closest to a demo-ready flow.
