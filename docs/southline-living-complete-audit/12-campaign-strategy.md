# Campaign Strategy

## Core campaign idea

Get discovered by homeowners near you.

## Campaign promise

A contractor can publish a professional presence, receive local leads, and manage the first step of the customer relationship from one simple workspace.

## Campaign hooks

- Local discoverability.
- Faster lead capture.
- Cleaner professional presentation.
- Better trust and conversion.
- A simpler path from inquiry to estimate.

## Campaign audience

- homeowners actively planning projects;
- local contractors who need leads;
- operators or resellers who want a branded local-services platform.

## Campaign channel recommendations

| Channel | Fit | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct outreach to local contractors | Very high | 1 |
| Email outreach with a simple demo | High | 2 |
| Local SEO / content pages | High | 3 |
| Paid search for target services | Medium | 4 |
| Social proof / case studies | Medium | 5 |
| Referral partnerships | Medium | 6 |

## Campaign sequence

1. Lead with the local discovery promise.
2. Show the homeowner booking path.
3. Show the contractor lead board.
4. Explain the monthly professional package.
5. Offer a setup package and quick onboarding.

## Message framing

- Stop waiting for referrals.
- Be found when homeowners need you.
- Convert local interest into real leads.
- Make your profile, service offering, and booking path work together.

## CTA options

- Book a demo.
- Start your profile.
- Get listed.
- Launch your lead funnel.
- Request your setup package.

## Campaign risk

The strongest campaign language should avoid overclaiming broad marketplace liquidity or fully automated conversions. It should speak to the concrete present value: visibility, lead capture, and a simple workflow.
