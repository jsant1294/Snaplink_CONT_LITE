# Product Commercial Summary

## Product summary

The repository supports a compelling product story around lead generation, local discovery, contractor operations, and real-estate workflows. The strongest present-day proof points are the public booking flow, contractor lead board, CMS, and module entitlements.

## Commercial summary

The most commercially viable path is to position the platform as a professional lead-generation and local presence platform for home-service contractors, with optional add-on modules such as campaigns, Flipbooks, and later-stage financial tools.

## Best commercial narrative

- Homeowners can discover local professionals and request work.
- Contractors can receive leads and manage them in a shared workspace.
- Operators can manage content, modules, and product visibility.
- The platform can expand into real-estate operations over time.

## Premium selling points

- local discovery,
- lead capture,
- professional workspace,
- campaign add-ons,
- operator control,
- CRM-like real-estate operations.

## Avoid overclaiming

Do not frame the product as a fully matured, fully integrated marketplace or as a complete end-to-end enterprise software suite without more product hardening.
