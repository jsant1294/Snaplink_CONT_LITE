# Real-Estate Reality Check

## What is real

The repository clearly contains a real-estate operations architecture. It includes routes, schemas, and UI surfaces for agents, brokerages, buyers, sellers, leads, transactions, properties, communications, tasks, showings, open houses, campaigns, and analytics.

## What is strong

- The architecture is broad and credible.
- The data model is more mature than a simple brochure site.
- The platform can plausibly support real CRM-like workflows.

## What is not yet proven

- The platform is not clearly proven as a fully live MLS or IDX integration engine.
- Some section routes appear placeholder-like or staged.
- The system looks more like a robust internal operations platform than a fully matured public marketplace with real live listing feeds.

## Commercial implication

The real-estate layer is strong enough to support a B2B sales narrative around CRM and workflow operations, but it should not be sold as a fully integrated MLS/IDX replacement until those integrations are tested and proven.

## Recommended positioning

Position the real-estate layer as an operations-first platform for agents and brokerages, not as a turnkey marketplace competitor from day one.
